Basic Help

  1. Open Open Speech ASR from the menu bar.
  2. Grant Microphone permission when prompted.
  3. Grant Input Monitoring permission if you want global shortcuts to work while another app is active.
  4. Record speech from the app menu or your configured shortcut.
  5. After transcription, the result is copied to the clipboard. Paste it into your target app with Command-V.
